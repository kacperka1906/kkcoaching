## Rules for future content

- Do not create additional city landing pages unless Search Console data shows real demand and each page can contain genuinely local, unique content.
- The exact local head term **personal trainer Cwmbran** belongs primarily to `/personal-training-cwmbran`, not the homepage.
- The homepage should remain a brand and service overview rather than a second copy of the local PT landing page.
- New informational articles should link naturally to the single commercial page that best matches the user's next step.
- Polish pages should target Polish-language search intent in the UK rather than literal translations of English keywords.
