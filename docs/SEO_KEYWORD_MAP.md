## Keyword use rules from this research

- `/personal-training-cwmbran` owns the local PT cluster: **personal trainer Cwmbran**, **personal training Cwmbran**, **fitness coach Cwmbran** and local weight-loss support.
- `/online-coaching` owns the remote coaching cluster: **online coaching**, **online personal trainer**, **online fitness coach** and **PT online** variants.
- `/training-plan` owns the plan cluster: **training plan**, **workout plan**, **gym workout plan**, **gym training plan**, **fitness plan** and **personalised training plan**.
- Broad phrases such as **weight loss**, **lose weight**, **weight loss plan** and **fat loss** are valuable, but they should not be crammed into every commercial page. Use them as natural outcome language now and build deeper informational content around them later.
- Ignore irrelevant Bing related-keyword suggestions such as weight-loss pills, injections, tablets, Boots/NHS navigational searches or unrelated brands. Search volume alone is not enough; intent must match KK Coaching services.
- Do not create separate exact-match pages like `/weight-loss-cwmbran`, `/fitness-coach-cwmbran` or `/lose-weight-cwmbran` unless future Search Console data shows enough real demand and each page can contain genuinely unique content.
- Internal links should reinforce intent: local PT pages link to Hybrid and Training Plan where useful; Online Coaching links to the one-off Training Plan for users who do not need ongoing support.
